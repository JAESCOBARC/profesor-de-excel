What's inside

What each Budget Plan Pro module contains

Each module is a screen of the app, connected to the others. Open any of them to see what you enter and what you get.

Parameters

One of the main sheets: this is where the plan's general settings are configured.

  • Plan time horizon: from 5 up to 10 years.
  • Language: Spanish, English or Portuguese.
  • Currency used across the whole app and the reports.
  • Corporate income tax rate applied when calculating the result.

Planning

The strategic part of your business plan, organized in six tabs.

  • Strategic direction: the project's mission, vision and goals.
  • SWOT: each factor has a weight (each quadrant adds up to 100%) and a score from 0 to 10; the quadrant score is the weighted average. You can benchmark against up to 2 competitors.
  • Buyer persona: up to 4 customer profiles.
  • Sales funnel: the stages a customer goes through until they buy.
  • Market research: TAM, SAM, SOM, competition and PESTEL analysis.
  • Timeline: set a start and end date, add activities with their start, end and % complete, and the app draws the Gantt chart.

Investments

The assets you need to buy to start and run the business.

  • What you buy, when, and what each asset costs.
  • The app calculates depreciation and its effect on the balance sheet and cash flow.

Financing

How you pay for those investments and for the business.

  • Equity and loans.
  • The app calculates the loan schedule and its effect on the balance sheet and cash flow.

Revenue

What you sell and at what price.

  • Up to 10 products or services.
  • For each year: net selling price and units sold, with the total.
  • Days of customer credit, which affect when you get paid.

Purchases and Stock

The purchases of goods tied to your sales.

  • Their cost is one of the variables you can move in the sensitivity analysis (purchase cost).

Staff

The project team.

  • The staff data you enter feeds into costs, the income statement and cash flow.

Operating Expenses

The running costs of the business.

  • They are added to costs and show up in the income statement and in cash flow.

Cost Statement

A summary of the project's costs, year by year.

  • It is calculated automatically from what you entered in purchases, staff and expenses.

Income Statement (P&L)

The profit and loss statement over 5-10 years, calculated by the app.

  • Includes EBITDA, EBIT and net result.
  • Expandable rows with "Expand all" and "Collapse all" to see the detail.

Cash Flow Statement

The money coming in and going out, and how much you have available.

  • Opening balance, total receipts, total payments, period balance and available cash, year by year.
  • With a start row (START) before the first year.

Balance Sheet

The financial position of the project, year by year.

  • Accounting validation checks that the balance sheet balances, and the alerts panel warns you if it doesn't.

Cash Flow and KPIs

The answer to whether the project is viable.

  • IRR and NPV (with real discounting), with the viability criterion shown.
  • Break-even point: the minimum sales required.
  • Net cash flow and ratios by period: working capital, liquidity, leverage, asset turnover, operating margin, ROA and ROE.
  • Automatic analysis with traffic lights that explains what changes from one year to the next.
  • Accounting check: the maximum imbalance in the plan.

Sensitivity and Scenarios

What happens to your plan if something changes.

  • Controls for average selling price, volume, purchase cost and interest rate: IRR and NPV recalculate instantly.
  • A 5 × 5 price × volume sensitivity table.
  • Save pessimistic, base and optimistic scenarios and compare them.

Charts

Your plan as visuals, ready to present.

  • Sales vs. costs, P&L waterfall, cumulative cash flow, break-even point and KPIs.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered

What is Budget Plan Pro?

Budget Plan Pro is Windows software that works as a business plan template without needing Excel: it is not a spreadsheet. It turns your investment, sales, cost and staffing data into a complete business plan and a 5-10 year financial plan: income statement, cash flow, balance sheet, IRR, NPV and break-even point. Everything is calculated automatically, and the result can be exported to PDF, Word or Excel to share.

Is it an Excel business plan template?

No, and that is the main difference. Budget Plan Pro is a standalone program with the financial model already built and tested: you do not work on cells or formulas, you just enter your data on its screens. You do not need Excel installed. The Excel export exists only so you can share the result with whoever asks for it.

How do you write a business plan without fighting with Excel?

By no longer building it in a spreadsheet. With Budget Plan Pro you enter your data in an app that already has every calculation done, and you get your financial statements instantly without writing a single formula. If someone later asks for the file, you export it to Excel in one click.

Is there a free Excel business plan template that actually works?

There are free templates that are useful to get started, but they usually cover only part of the plan, require you to understand the formulas and break when you add rows or years. Budget Plan Pro is designed for people who need the complete plan, balanced and presentable, without spending days on a spreadsheet.

What is a financial evaluation of an investment project?

It is the analysis that answers whether a project is worth it: it compares the investment with the cash flows it generates and calculates indicators such as NPV (net present value), IRR (internal rate of return) and the break-even point. Budget Plan Pro calculates all three automatically from your data.

How do you create an investment plan?

An investment plan lists the assets you need to buy, when and how much they cost, how they are financed (equity or loans) and how they are depreciated. In Budget Plan Pro you fill it in under the Investments and Financing sections, and the app generates the depreciation and loan schedule and its effect on the balance sheet and cash flow.

Do I need to know finance or accounting?

No. You only need to know your business: what you will sell, at what price, what it costs you and what you need to buy. The app does the calculations, checks that the balance sheet balances, warns you about inconsistencies and includes help for each section and a complete sample project.
